## Why it matters
Dove Beach is the game's second hand-made city: a laid-back beach town on hills whose roads are secretly designed to
be fun to drive, and whose road outline from above is a dove (read kotlin-street/docs/CITY2.md in full first). Claude
built its road network (`src/town/city2/`: align.ts, site.ts, roads.ts, ground.ts). The town itself (the lots, the
buildings and how it grows through the eras) isn't planned yet. That is your kind of work: deterministic TypeScript
planning, like the capital's plat (src/town/plat.ts, districts.ts, docs: kotlin-street/docs/TOWN_PLAN.md).

## What to build (in `src/town/city2/`, new files)
1. **Lots along curved and sloped streets:** frontage lots that follow the road centrelines of `roads.ts` (not an axis
   grid), sized by district (CITY2.md's districts: Old Harbour, Old Town on the Crown's slope, Sallow Mills, Seagrove's
   beach cottages, Fellside, the Ridge villas, Eastcove's marina, Heron Marsh), with front and back yards, never on a
   road, a grade too steep to build on, the beach or the marsh.
2. **The era ledger:** what stands on each lot in each year from the 1700s to the 2050s, following CITY2.md's history
   (the fort first, the harbour, the 1886 railway, the 1905 Seagrove grid, the resort years, post-war Fellside, the
   marina...), using the capital's building kinds where they fit (src/town/kinds.ts), so `standingIn(year)` lists every
   building with its lot, kind and era.
3. **Landmark plots** reserved for CITY2.md's landmarks (the Crown observatory, the Strand pier and pavilion, the Head
   Light, Neck Fort, the Dove Fountain, the Custom House...) by their dates.
4. **Keep the dove:** nothing you add may move a road; the existing test that holds the outline must stay green.

## Tests
- Every lot fronts a road, sits off the carriageway and on buildable ground; no two lots overlap.
- The ledger is deterministic from the seed and follows the history (no marina before it was built, etc.).
- A top-down map of lots by era for 1900, 1960 and 2025 (PNG or SVG, in your delivery).
- `tsc` clean; the city2 and capital tests stay green.
