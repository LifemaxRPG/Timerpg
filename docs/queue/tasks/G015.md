# G015: From the cell to the courtroom: serious arrests go to trial

- Assignee: gemini
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `gemini/G015-loop-court`
- Status: hold (Claude opens it once G012 and G013 are accepted)

## Why it matters
The loop's `arrest()` ends every case with a fine or a night in a cell. Your court is ready. This sends serious arrests
to it (your G010 idea).

**The player's first encounter:** arrested for the burglary on Elm Street, they're offered bail. They can afford only a
public defender. A month later the jury is out for two rounds before it comes back.

## What to build
- Petty offences (severity below about 0.35) keep the fast fine-or-cell path.
- Felonies and contested arrests go to `src/court/`: charges, bail, a plea offer, choosing a lawyer by the player's
  money, the trial on the arraignment date, then the verdict and the sentence.
- Each decision point is a `TimerpgSim` call the game can show as a choice: plead or go to trial, which lawyer, pay bail
  or wait in jail. Document each in docs/LOOP_API.md with an example.
- A sentence moves the player's clock (jail days); an acquittal frees them.

## Tests
- A petty theft still ends in a fine.
- A burglary arrest goes to trial and returns a verdict and a sentence through `TimerpgSim`.
- A plea deal shortens the sentence.
- Save/load works mid-trial (between arraignment and the trial date).
