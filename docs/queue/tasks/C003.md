## Brief
A small-town ballpark: a wooden grandstand behind home plate (covered, about 600 seats), bleachers down
the lines, dugouts, an outfield fence with painted made-up local ads, a scoreboard, a concession stand and ticket
booth, light towers (from 1950), and a gravel lot.
States: 1935-1959 wooden; 1960-1999 lights and steel bleachers; 2000+ renovated (aluminium seats, new scoreboard).
