- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: model
- Branch: `codex/C048-tractor`

## Why it matters
The farms outside the capital have a tractor parked in the yard, but it's a crude stand-in: a blue box on wheels. The
owner asked for a higher-quality tractor in your style, like your cars.

## Brief
An **original** farm tractor (no real makers, logos or colour schemes that identify a real brand), in two eras:
1. **1940s-1950s:** a small row-crop tractor: narrow front wheels close together, big ribbed rear tyres, an open
   seat, a tall exhaust stack, exposed engine sides, a steering wheel on a long column.
2. **1970s-1980s:** a bigger utility tractor: a cab with glass, wider front axle, a three-point hitch at the back,
   lights on the cab roof.

## Must have
- **The vehicle rules in C011** (docs/queue/tasks/C011.md, "Vehicle rules"): nodes, lights, paint mask, collision,
  mid and far models, and the review sheet. Wheels as separate nodes so they can turn.
- **Same pixel-art texture style and texel size as your cars** (`texel_m` 0.0388).
- **model-info.json** with mass, wheelbase, track, tyre sizes front and rear, and a top speed of the era (slow).
- **Paint options:** a few plausible farm colours, plus a worn, faded version for old farms.
- **Review sheet:** both eras side by side, a low three-quarter view, from behind, and next to one of your cars for
  scale.
