# C020: The fence (a back-room buyer for stolen goods)

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C020-fence`
- Status: accepted

## Why it matters
The playable loop lets the player steal and sell at the pawn shop, but a pawnbroker records serial numbers, and stolen
goods can be traced back (the crime code does this). A real thief goes to a fence instead: someone who pays less, keeps
no records, and has to be found first. Your economy already has `fenceOffer`. This task makes the fence a person in the
town.

**The player's first encounter:** the pawnbroker turns away a watch that's too hot. A regular at the diner, who has
heard who the player is, says "try Vic, behind the hardware store, after dark." Vic pays $4 where the pawn shop would
have paid $9, and asks nothing.

## What to build: `src/economy/fence.ts`, pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Who fences in a town:** from a list of people (id, age, traits such as honesty, greed and sociability, and job) and
   a seed, pick 0 to 2 fences by traits and town size. Each gets a cover (a job or a place), a where-and-when (a back
   door, a bar, after dark), and the categories they'll take.
2. **How the player finds one:** `fenceLeads(fences, people, seed)` lists which other people know of each fence and
   how willing they'd be to say. The game turns that into dialogue facts; you return plain facts such as "knows Vic
   fences goods behind the hardware store".
3. **Dealing:** `fenceDeal(fence, itemId, year, hot, trust)` uses your `fenceOffer`, adjusted by the fence's greed and
   how far they trust the player (trust grows with each deal). A fence refuses what they don't handle, and won't touch
   anything too hot until the player is trusted.
4. **The risk:** a fence can be an informant by era and traits. `fenceRisk(fence, year)` gives the chance a deal ends
   up in a police report, which the game passes on to the crime system.
5. Plain JSON in and out, documented in docs/ECONOMY.md with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs, no
   `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/`).

## Tests (Vitest)
- Fences are deterministic by seed; a small town can have none.
- A fence pays less than the pawn shop for the same item.
- A fence refuses categories they don't handle, and very hot goods until trusted.
- Leads come from people whose traits make sense.
- Informant risk varies by era and traits.
- The C018 and C019 tests stay green.
