# C038: Locks, alarms and watchdogs (how safe a building is)

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C038-security`
- Status: open

## Why it matters
Break-ins are in the game (a closed shop, a house), and your skills module has lockpicking and safecracking. What a
building is secured with should come from the building, the owner and the era: a farmhouse door never locked in 1925, a
deadbolt in 1975, a downtown jeweller's alarm wired to the police, a dog in the yard.

**The player's first encounter:** the back door of the hardware store, 1952: a simple pin-tumbler lock (difficulty
0.3), no alarm, but the owner's dog sleeps in the storeroom.

## What to build: `src/security/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
- `securityOf(building, year, seed)`: for a building the game supplies (kind, owner wealth, neighbourhood, contents
  value), return its doors and windows with locks (none, latch, pin tumbler, deadbolt, by era), whether they're left
  unlocked (by era and place), alarms (local bell, then a monitored alarm by era), a safe (using your skills module's
  `safeProfile`), and dogs.
- `entryOptions(security, skills)`: ways in (an unlocked door, picking, forcing, a window), each with a difficulty for
  your skills module's `attempt`, plus what forcing leaves behind (noise, damage, evidence).
- `alarmResponse(alarm, year)`: who comes and how fast (a bell neighbours hear, the police by era).
- Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/SECURITY.md`. Import from `src/skills/`; don't edit it. No Node or
  browser APIs, no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand`).

## Tests
- A 1925 farmhouse is often unlocked; a 1985 jeweller's shop isn't.
- Monitored alarms appear only in their era.
- Forcing leaves damage and noise.
- Determinism.
- At least one test per bullet above.
