## Why it matters
Dove Beach (kotlin-street/docs/CITY2.md) is the game's beach town. The owner wants it advertised along the highways the
way real beach resorts are: big cheerful billboards that make you want to go, so a player crossing the continent keeps
seeing them and eventually drives there. The countryside already has a billboard model and one plain "Visit Dove Beach"
sign; this replaces it with a proper campaign.

## Brief
A set of **original** billboard faces (pixel-art pictures) for Dove Beach, in the game's style:
- **happy beach scenes:** families on the sand, kids with buckets, a couple on the pier at sunset, surfers, the
  lighthouse, the Dove Fountain, shrimp boats, ice cream on the Strand;
- **the town's look:** the dove crest (a dove over three waves), the beach town's pastel colours, its slogan(s) of your
  own (e.g. "Follow the doves to Dove Beach");
- **by era:** at least three campaigns that look of their time: 1920s–30s railway-poster style, 1950s–60s motor-age
  postcard style with a family car, and 1990s–2020s modern; each with 3–4 faces;
- **distance signs:** a smaller family of "Dove Beach — 120 miles / 45 / 12 / Next exit" boards in each era's style,
  so the game can count down as you approach.

## Must have
- **Pictures sized for the existing billboard** (ask nothing of the geometry): give the pixel size and texel size you
  used, matched to the countryside's billboard (kotlin-street/shared/src/filament/kotlin/scene3d/WorldPropKit.kt).
- **No real brands, places or people;** the families are generic characters in the game's pixel style.
- **Weathered versions** of two faces (sun-faded, peeling), for old boards still standing in later eras.
- **Review sheet:** every face at full size, and a few on the billboard model beside a country road.
