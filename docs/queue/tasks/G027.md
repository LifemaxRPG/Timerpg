## Why it matters
Your idea from the survey, taken: paying in 1952 with a 2025 bill, wearing a 1920s suit in 2025, or pawning a watch
whose serial number hasn't been made yet shouldn't throw a sci-fi error. People react like real people, and a careless
time traveller quietly builds a reputation as a strange outsider before the police ever match a fingerprint.

## What to build (in `src/loop/`)
1. **Every item and garment knows its era** (made in, in style from, until), from G026's player state.
2. **Out-of-era checks** when the player pays, sells, pawns, shows papers or is looked at closely: a bill not yet
   printed, a coin of the wrong design, a device that doesn't exist yet, clothes decades out of style; how odd it is
   scales with the gap and the item.
3. **Real reactions:** the clerk refuses the bill ("That bill looks funny, pal, try the bank"), the pawnbroker asks
   where you got it, people stare at the suit; plain-English facts for the HUD.
4. **Memory and gossip:** each tell becomes a memory (your G021 `memoriesFor`) and spreads like any rumour, building an
   "odd outsider" reputation in that town and era; enough of it makes the police curious, never certain.
5. Plain JSON for the bridge; document in `docs/LOOP_API.md`.

## Tests
- A 2025 bill in 1952 is refused and remembered; a 1952 bill in 1952 is fine.
- A 1920s suit in 2025 draws remarks; changing clothes stops them.
- Tells spread by gossip with the right hedging; the police get curious only past a threshold.
