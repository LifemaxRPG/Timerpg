## Brief
An original roadside filling station, distinct from our existing Crossfield station. States:
1. **1925-1945:** a cottage-style station with a small canopy, two visible-globe gravity pumps, an oil rack and an air post.
2. **1945-1975:** an enamel-panel box station with two service bays (lifts, tyres), a canopy over two pump islands, a tall pole sign with prices, and a pay phone.
3. **1975 onward:** a self-serve convenience store with a wide canopy, six pumps, an LED price sign, an ice chest and an air machine. Or a closed version with the canopy kept and the pumps removed.

Must have:
- the pump nozzles as anchor points (the game will let the player refuel);
- the service bay doors as nodes;
- the cashier counter anchors.
