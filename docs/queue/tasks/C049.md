# C049: Driftwood "boneyard" trees for Dove Beach (the owner's request)

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: model
- Branch: `codex/C049-boneyard-trees`
- Status: open

## Why it matters
Dove Beach is the game's second hand-made city: a laid-back beach town (docs: kotlin-street/docs/CITY2.md). The owner
wants the big dead trees you find on some Southeast US beaches, where the shoreline has eaten into a maritime forest:
whole oaks and cedars killed by salt, bleached silver-grey by the sun, standing or fallen on the sand, roots exposed,
some reaching into the surf at high tide. They're one of the most striking sights on that coast, and they'll make the
beach unmistakable.

## Brief
A set of **original** driftwood trees (natural objects; no real named place is depicted):
- **at least 8 variants:** 3 standing dead trees (a wide spreading live oak skeleton, a tall cedar snag, a leaning
  pine), 3 fallen trunks (one with the root plate standing up, one half-buried in sand, one long trunk with branch
  stubs), and 2 stumps or root plates on their own;
- **sun-bleached** silver-grey and pale driftwood tones, with darker wet bases where the tide reaches, barnacle or
  weed tints low down on one variant, and a little sand drift at the base;
- **the shapes tell the story:** twisted, wind-shaped limbs, no leaves, smooth weathered wood, broken branch ends,
  exposed tangled roots;
- **a few small pieces** to scatter around them: driftwood logs, branches, a shell line.

## Must have
- **The game's style:** pixel-art textures on simple low-poly geometry, as your other models, at the same texel size
  (`texel_m` 0.0388), nearest-filtered, lit and shadowed.
- **Real scale:** the big oak skeleton about 8-10 m across, the cedar snag about 9-12 m tall, fallen trunks 6-12 m.
- **Collision** boxes for trunks and big limbs (the player can climb on the fallen ones), none for thin branches.
- **Mid and far models** for each (the beach is seen from the roads above it), as the vehicle rules in C011 describe
  for cars.
- **model-info.json** listing each variant with its size, and a suggested placement (dry sand, wet sand, in the surf).
- **Review sheet:** all variants on a sand ground in a row with the player for scale, a low beach view at sunset, and
  a group of them arranged as a "boneyard" stretch of beach.
