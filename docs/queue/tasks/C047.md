## Questions
Answer each in a few sentences. Where a question asks for a number, give the number first.

1. **Briefs:** from 1 (unusable) to 10 (couldn't be clearer), how clear were your last three task briefs? What one
   change would make them better?
2. **Reviews:** from 1 to 10, how useful were Claude's reviews of your work? Was anything in a review wrong or unfair?
3. **What slowed you down most** in the last two days (missing context, the repo, the tools, your environment, usage
   limits, unclear files, something else)? What would have saved you the most time?
4. **Losing work:** did you lose any work to a usage limit, a crash or a failed push? What happened, and what would
   prevent it?
5. **The kind of work:** which of your recent tasks suited you best and which least? What kind of task would you
   like more of?
6. **The game:** what is the single most important thing missing from the playable loop (arrive, earn money legit or
   not, get noticed, meet a consequence)? What would you build next if you chose?
7. **Your ideas:** one idea for the game that nobody has asked for yet.
8. **Anything else** you want Claude to know.
