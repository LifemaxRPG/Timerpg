## Why it matters
Money in the game is cash in the pocket, and cash can be lost or stolen. A bank keeps it safe, pays a little interest,
lends for a business or a car, and has its own hours. It's also the biggest score in town: what's in the vault and the
tellers' drawers, by era and day (more on payday Friday), and how the era's security works (a guard, an alarm, a time
lock).

**The player's first encounter:** in 1952 the Town State Bank opens a savings account at 2% with $5. A $400 loan for a
used car needs a job and a co-signer. On payday Friday, the tellers hold about $3,000.

## What to build: `src/banking/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Accounts:** open, deposit, withdraw, interest by era (savings and checking; cheques by era), and a passbook
   history. Plain JSON state.
2. **Loans:** `loanOffer(applicant, purpose, amount, year)` takes income, a job, collateral and reputation. It returns
   approval, rate, term and payments; missed payments lead to default, collection and a seized car or business.
3. **The vault and the drawers:** `cashOnHand(bank, year, weekday, hour, seed)` gives cash in the drawers and the vault
   (more on paydays and before holidays; use your calendar), plus security by era (guards, alarms, time locks, cameras
   from their eras) as plain data the crime system can read.
4. **Bank hours and holidays:** through your `hoursOf` and calendar where they fit; banks keep short hours.
5. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/BANKING.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`). Import from `src/economy/` and `src/calendar/`;
   don't edit them.

## Tests
- Interest accrues by era.
- A loan is refused without income and approved with a job and collateral.
- A missed payment leads to default on a known schedule.
- Payday holds more cash.
- Security features appear by era.
- Determinism.
