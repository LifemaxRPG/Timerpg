# G016: The sky in Filament (a pixel-art sky dome)

- Assignee: gemini
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `gemini/G016-sky`
- Status: accepted

## Why it matters
Today the town is drawn by Filament on a transparent surface, over a separate 2D sky painted by the old renderer
(`kotlin-street/shared/src/main/kotlin/scene/Backdrop.kt`, colours from `SkyColors` in `scene/ScenePalette.kt`). Every
frame the phone blends the two full-screen. Moving the sky into Filament removes that blend, frees the old renderer for
deletion (docs/CLEANUP_PLAN.md, step 2), and lets the sky light the world properly. It's a graphics task on the scale
of your ocean (G002), in the same pixel-art style.

**The player's first encounter:** standing on Main Street at dusk, the sky fades in hard pixel bands from orange to
deep blue. The first stars come out over the rooftops, and a crescent moon rises behind the clock tower.

## What to build (new files only)
1. **A sky material** `kotlin-street/filament/materials/sky.mat`, compiled to
   `kotlin-street/app/src/main/assets/filament/sky.filamat` the same way `street_lit.mat` is built
   (kotlin-street/filament/TRIAL.md). Unlit, drawn behind everything (depth at the far plane), on a dome or a
   full-screen pass that follows the camera.
2. **The look, from the old sky's logic.** Read `Backdrop.kt` and `SkyColors`, and match their behaviour by time of day,
   weather and season:
   - stepped colour bands (no smooth gradients; pixel-art banding);
   - the sun and the moon as pixel-art discs, at the positions the old sky uses;
   - the moon's phase from the game's date;
   - stars at night that fade in at dusk, stable as the camera turns (fixed to the sky, not the screen);
   - clouds in pixel art that drift, more of them when overcast;
   - mist and rain dimming the sky.
   Nearest-filtered textures only.
3. **A CPU-side `SkyState`** (time, weather, season to colours, sun and moon direction, phase, star brightness), so the
   game's lighting and the ocean (your `OceanSurface`: sky reflection and moon glint) can use the same values. Plain
   Kotlin, no allocation per frame.
4. **A desktop preview** `-Dmode=sky` in a NEW file `kotlin-street/desktop/src/filament/kotlin/FilamentSkyPreview.kt`:
   - dawn, noon, dusk and midnight;
   - clear, overcast, rain and mist;
   - a full moon and a crescent;
   - the camera turned 90° (stars and clouds must stay put).

## Acceptance
- New files only, plus registering the preview mode in `kotlin-street/desktop/build.gradle.kts` if needed. Do not change

- Renders of each preview shot in `prototypes/sky/`, stated as desktop renders, plus one contact sheet. If your sandbox
