# C009: Police station and town jail

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: model
- Branch: `codex/C009-police-station`
- Status: accepted

## Why it matters
The team's next milestone is the first playable crime loop (docs/queue/TEAM.md). When the player is caught, this is
where they're booked and held. Jeff's witness system feeds its case files. Make it a place the player remembers.

## Brief
One original police station for the capital (a made-up town name on the sign, repaintable), through the eras:
1. **1900-1939:** a brick and stone station with the town jail behind it.
   - A front desk with a tall sergeant's counter, and a chief's office.
   - A cell block of 3-4 barred cells with bunks, a bucket and a high barred window, and a heavy iron door between the
     office and the cells.
   - A hitching rail, a lamp over the door with a "POLICE" globe, and a wanted-poster board by the door (blank paper
     panels the game fills).
2. **1940-1979:** the same building, modernised.
   - Radio mast on the roof, a squad-car lot with a garage bay at the back, a booking room (a height chart wall,
     a fingerprint counter, a camera stand), and an interview room with a table and a one-way window.
3. **1980 onward:** a renovated or extended station.
   - A new glass entrance vestibule, a holding area with modern cells (solid doors with a small window), CCTV
     cameras as small nodes, a sally port for transport vans, a records room, and a parking lot with a fence and gate.

## Must have
- **Cells:** each cell is a separate room with its door as a named node (`cell_door_<n>`) that the game opens and
  locks, and a floor node.
  - Mark the escape-relevant features as nodes or in model-info: windows with bars, vents, the yard fence and the roof
    hatch. Prison-break mechanics come later.
- **Collision:** walls, the counters and cell bars, as boxes listed in model-info.json.
- **Anchors** in model-info.json:
  - the front-desk officer's spot, the booking spot, each cell's bunk;
  - the interview chair, and the car spots in the lot.
- **Night:** a lit entrance globe, the lit front-desk windows, and a dim cell block.
