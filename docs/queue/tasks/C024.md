# C024: Fares and timetables (getting around without a car)

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C024-transit`
- Status: open

## Why it matters
The game has no fast travel. The player travels the world at its real scale, by road, rail and sea. Without a car, they
need streetcars, buses, taxis and trains, with fares and timetables that fit the era. That's also a cost of living, and
a way to leave town in a hurry after a crime.

**The player's first encounter:** in 1925 the streetcar on Main Street runs every 12 minutes for a nickel. The last one
leaves at 11:40 p.m. The night train to the next town is $1.85, third class, at 10:15.

## What to build: `src/transit/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Modes by era:** horsecar, streetcar, motor bus, taxi, interurban, railway (classes by era), and later a city bus
   and an intercity coach. Each has the years it runs and how it's priced (flat fare, by distance, or a taxi meter).
2. **Fares:** `fare(mode, year, distanceKm, class?)` in integer cents, using the economy's `priceIndex` (import it from
   `src/economy/`) and your own curves where transit priced differently.
3. **Timetables:** `departures(route, year, weekday, fromHour, seed)` gives the next departures from headways by time of
   day (rush, midday, evening, owl service by era) and first and last runs. Railway routes take a few fixed trains a day.
4. **Journey time:** `travelTime(mode, year, distanceKm)` by era (speeds, stops), so the game can run the ride in real
   time.
5. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/TRANSIT.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- No buses before their era.
- The streetcar fare is a nickel-ish in 1925 (state your range).
- Rush hour runs more often than midnight.
- The last run exists.
- Railway classes are priced in order.
- Determinism.
