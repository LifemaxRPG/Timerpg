## Why it matters
The owner's decision: every building in the game has a real address that can be searched on the GPS, found on a map
or in a directory, written on a letter, given by a person, and read off a house number in the street. The game opens
with the professor's letter: "Come to the capital, to this address." Today nothing has an address.

## What to build (deterministic TypeScript, new module `src/address/`)
1. **Street names** for every street of every town (the capital, generated towns, Dove Beach's named roads): unique
   within a town, fitting the town's region and era of founding (trees, founders, numbered streets in grids, "Old ...
   Road" for country lanes), stable through the eras except where a street is renamed in history (record renames with
   the year).
2. **House numbers** the way real towns number them: odd on one side, even on the other, rising away from the town's
   origin, gaps for empty lots, "1/2" and unit letters for split buildings, apartment and unit numbers inside blocks.
3. **Postal codes** per town and district, appearing in the era they were introduced (none before then).
4. **Lookup both ways:** `addressOf(buildingId, year)` and `find(query, year)` for the GPS and directories (partial
   names, typos, "the diner on Main"), plus "nearest address" to a world position. Plain JSON in and out, for the
   phone's bridge.
5. **Era directories:** a printable city directory / phone book for a town and year (who lives and works at each
   address, from the sim), for the paper-map eras.
Use the existing town plan data (src/town/: plat, districts, the street graph; src/town/city2/roads.ts for Dove Beach;
the baked generated towns from kotlin-street/tools/towns-bake.ts) without changing how towns are planned.

## Tests
- Every building in the capital and a generated town has exactly one address per year; no two share one.
- Odd/even sides and rising numbers hold on every street; numbering is stable across years for buildings that stand.
- `find` returns the right building for exact, partial and misspelled queries.
- Deterministic from the seed; `tsc` clean; existing town tests stay green.
