# G013: From the cell to the courtroom: arrests that go to trial, and fairer lawyers

- Assignee: gemini
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `gemini/G013-loop-court`
- Status: open (start after G012 is delivered, since both touch `src/loop/`)

## Why it matters
Your court (G010) is merged. The loop's `arrest()` still ends every case with a fine or a night in a cell. This task
sends serious arrests to your court, as you suggested, and tunes one thing from the review: how much money decides a
verdict.

**The player's first encounter:** arrested for the burglary on Elm Street, they're offered bail. They can afford only
a public defender. A month later the jury is out for two rounds before it comes back.

## What to build
1. **Arrests into court** (your G010 idea).
   - Petty offences (severity below about 0.35) keep the fast fine-or-cell path.
   - Felonies and contested arrests go to `src/court/`: charges, bail, a plea offer, choosing a lawyer by the player's
     money, the trial on the arraignment date, then the verdict and the sentence.
   - Expose each decision point through `TimerpgSim`: plead or go to trial, which lawyer, pay bail or wait in jail.
     Each is a plain JSON call the game can show as a choice.
   - A sentence moves the player's clock (jail days); an acquittal frees them.
2. **Fairer lawyers.** On the contested case in your test, money currently decides the verdict outright: the public
   defender always loses, elite counsel always wins. Tune it so that:
   - a good lawyer clearly helps;
   - on a weak case, a public defender sometimes wins;
   - on a strong case with physical evidence, even elite counsel usually loses.
   Show the spread over 60 seeds for a weak, a contested and a strong case.
3. **Crime kinds for court crimes.** Add `bribery`, `perjury` and `intimidation` to `CrimeKind` (a small, listed change
   in `src/crime/`), with severities and report behaviour, and use them in place of the `theft`/`assault` stand-ins.

## Conventions
- Keep `TimerpgSim`'s existing names; add new methods. Every call is plain JSON (docs/LOOP_API.md: document each new
  one with an example).
- Ids are mapped only in `src/loop/ids.ts`.
- Timing tests go in their own file.

## Tests
- A petty theft still ends in a fine.
- A burglary arrest goes to trial and returns a verdict and a sentence through `TimerpgSim`.
- A plea deal shortens the sentence.
- The lawyer spread for the three cases.
- Bribing a juror opens a `bribery` case.
- Save/load works mid-trial (between arraignment and the trial date).
