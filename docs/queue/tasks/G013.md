# G013: Fairer lawyers, and crime kinds for court crimes

- Assignee: gemini
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `gemini/G013-court-tuning`
- Status: accepted (review r1)

## Why it matters
Your court (G010) is merged. One thing from the review needs tuning: how much money decides a verdict. Court crimes
also need their own crime kinds. This task touches only `src/court/` and `src/crime/`, so it doesn't depend on any
other open task.

**The player's first encounter:** with only a public defender and a thin case against them, the jury is out for two
rounds and comes back not guilty.

## What to build
1. **Fairer lawyers.** On the contested case in your test, money currently decides the verdict outright: the public
   defender always loses, elite counsel always wins. Tune it so that:
   - a good lawyer clearly helps;
   - on a weak case, a public defender sometimes wins;
   - on a strong case with physical evidence, even elite counsel usually loses.
   Show the spread over 60 seeds for a weak, a contested and a strong case.
2. **Crime kinds for court crimes.** Add `bribery`, `perjury` and `intimidation` to `CrimeKind` (a small, listed change
   in `src/crime/`), with severities and report behaviour, and use them in place of the `theft`/`assault` stand-ins.

## Tests
- The lawyer spread for the three cases.
- Bribing a juror opens a `bribery` case; perjury and intimidation open theirs.
- The G010 suite and the crime suite stay green.

## Allowed files
- `src/court/`, `docs/COURT.md`.
- `src/crime/types.ts` and the reporting tables, only for the three crime kinds; list the change.
- `prototypes/G013/DELIVERY.md`, with your self-review and ideas.
