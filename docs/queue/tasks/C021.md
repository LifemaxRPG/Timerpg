# C021: Opening hours by era

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C021-hours`
- Status: accepted

## Why it matters
Shops in the playable loop open by a fixed guess per kind. Hours shape crime and work: a store shut on Sunday in 1925,
a gas station open all night by 1975, a diner's early breakfast, Saturday half-days, late opening on payday. When a shop
is closed and empty is when someone breaks in.

## What to build: `src/economy/hours.ts`, pure and deterministic, with tests
- `hoursOf(shopKind, year, weekday, seed)` returns the opening spans for that day (or closed), shaped by era: Sunday
  closing laws early in the century, longer hours later, all-night places from about the 1960s-70s.
- `isOpen(shopKind, year, weekday, hour, seed)`.
- Holidays: a small authored list of days most shops close, by era.
- `staffed(shopKind, year, weekday, hour)` returns how many people are inside: the owner alone at opening, more at the
  midday rush, a night clerk.
- Plain JSON in and out, documented in docs/ECONOMY.md. No Node or browser APIs, no `Date.now` or `Math.random`.

## Tests
- A 1925 general store is shut on Sunday; a 1985 gas station is open at 3 a.m.
- Determinism by seed.
- Staffing peaks at the rush.
- The existing economy tests stay green.
