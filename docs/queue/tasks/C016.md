## Why it matters
Getting caught leads to trials and prison, and the owner wants prison-break mechanics inspired by The Escapist (docs/IDEAS.md). This is the prison: a **unique** landmark (`"naming": "fixed"`, a made-up name such as "Hollow Ridge State Penitentiary"). It is a big model, so prioritise a clean, complete first part over everything.

## Brief
A walled state penitentiary on open ground. States:
1. **1900-1960:** a stone cellhouse with tiers of cells along a central gallery, a high stone wall with corner guard towers, a yard, a mess hall, a laundry, an infirmary and the warden's office.
2. **1960 onward:** the same with added chain-link and razor wire, floodlight masts, a newer concrete wing, and a visitor room with glass and phones.

Must have, for the escape systems:
- every cell door as a node (`cell_<block>_<n>`) with bunk anchors;
- the yard, the mess hall, the laundry, the infirmary, the workshop, the warden's office and the gate as named rooms (each with a floor node);
- vents, drains, wall and fence lines, tower positions and the guard patrol path points as nodes or model-info data;
- collision for all walls and fences.

Budget: up to 40,000 triangles near (it's a destination), with mid and far models.
