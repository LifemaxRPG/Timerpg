## Why it matters
Your timeline engine (G017, made fast by G018) is the game's signature feature. This task lets the player actually
use it from the loop: step into the machine in the capital, pick a year, and arrive in a town that is what history
(and their own meddling) made it.

## What to build
1. **`jump(sim, { toYear })`:** returns the loop's state in the target year: the town from `presentAt`, the people
   alive then, their attitudes reset except where the timeline carries memory, police and open cases of that year.
2. **What travels with the player:** carried items (a receipt, a stolen watch) and the player themself, with your
   evidence-across-time rules (prints on file in 1990 matching a 1952 scene, and so on).
3. **Fuel:** the machine runs on nuclear material; the player starts with enough for about 4 trips (CLAUDE.md,
   docs/IDEAS.md). Each jump spends a unit; refuelling comes from the world later, not from this task.
4. **Loose threads:** `looseThreads` from the jump as plain-English facts the HUD and the NPC fact sheets can use.

## Tests
- Burn the store in 1952, jump to 2025: the lot is changed and the Gazette remembers.
- An item carried back and forth keeps its history; the prints match across the years they should.
- Fuel runs out after the last trip.
- A save taken after a jump round-trips.
