# G014: Booking, civilians who call the police, and small saves

- Assignee: gemini
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `gemini/G014-booking`
- Status: accepted (review r1)

## Why it matters
Three of your own ideas, plus a hook for Sam's fences, so the town reacts in more ways the player can trace.

**The player's first encounter:** they're booked for a 2-a.m. window break and fingerprinted. The next morning a
detective links the prints to a burglary from the year before, and the pawn shop's ledger puts a stolen watch in their
hands.

## What to build
1. **The booking pipeline (your G009 idea).** When an arrest completes with `transportedToStation`, book the player:
   - prints, and DNA where the era has it, go into G008 `identityFiles` via `enrollOnFile`;
   - G008's cold-case re-examination runs, so an old unsolved case can crack on the next tick;
   - `arrest()` returns what booking found, as plain-English facts for the game to show.
2. **Civilians call it in (your G011 idea).** `civilianSpotted(sim, personId, sighting)` and a `TimerpgSim` method
   for it: a person whose attitude is `report_on_sight` and who sees the player reports by the era's means (G005
   latency), which can send an officer.
3. **Small saves (your G011 idea).** Prune what resolved crimes no longer need (`toldPairs` and the like) at
   `arrest()` and `save()`. Show a 100-day save's size before and after.


4. **A fence who informs.** Sam's fences (`src/economy/fence.ts`) can be police informants (`fenceRisk`). Add an
   `act` kind `fence_sale` (or a `tipOff(sim, {informantId, itemId, crimeId?})` call) so an informant's tip reaches the
   police like a civilian's report. Today the game fakes it with a pawn-shop sale. Fences are people in your sim too,
   so their stance toward the player should follow from the deals.

## Tests
- Booking cracks a planted cold case.
- A shopkeeper who saw the theft spots the player two days later and calls it in, with the era's delay (1925 vs 1990).
- The pruned save round-trips and continues identically.
