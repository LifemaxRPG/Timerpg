## Why it matters
Dove Beach is the game's second hand-made city: a quiet, laid-back beach town on a bay, with hills behind it (read
kotlin-street/docs/CITY2.md). The town takes its name from a legend that its first settlers followed doves into the bay,
and seen from above its roads trace a dove diving toward the viewer. The owner wants an awesome dove landmark: the
city's signature, the thing on its postcards.

## Brief
An **original** dove monument that people would travel to see. Your design; some directions to weigh:
- **The Dove Fountain** at Fountain Circus, where the Esplanade meets the harbour: a large sculpted dove (or a flight
  of them) rising from the water, with water arcing from the wings, a ring of benches and lamps around it;
- or a tall **Dove Column** on the end of the harbour breakwater, a dove with spread wings at the top as a day mark
  for boats, lit at night;
- or something better of your own.

It should read as a dove at a glance from the street and from the hills above, feel of the place (a beach town
founded in the 1700s, grown through the resort years), and work in every era it stands in (give it a year it was
built, and say how it looks when new and when weathered).

## Must have
- **The landmark rules from your earlier landmarks** (the Meridian, the cinema): mid and far models, collision, night
  lighting, its plot size.
- **The game's style:** pixel-art textures on simple low-poly geometry, the same texel size as your other models,
  nearest-filtered, lit and shadowed; any water as simple shapes the engine can animate.
- **Review sheet:** street level, from above, at dusk and at night, and the player beside it for scale.

Work in `prototypes/<name>/` only. Write DELIVERY.md as the queue README says, with the Self-review from TEAM.md.
