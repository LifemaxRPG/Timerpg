## Why it matters
Your jobs module hires by age, references and reputation, but not by what the player can do. A mechanic's helper
should know engines; a night clerk should keep books; driving should need practice (and a licence from its era). Skills
give the player a reason to learn, and a slow path from odd jobs to a trade. They also feed crime: picking a lock, hot-
wiring a car, cracking a safe.

**The player's first encounter:** in 1952 the garage won't take the player as a mechanic's helper ("Ever worked on an
engine?"). Three evenings of a correspondence course and a week sweeping the garage floor later, they can.

## What to build: `src/skills/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Skills:** a small authored list (driving, mechanics, bookkeeping, cooking, carpentry, sales, first aid; and the
   shady ones: lockpicking, hot-wiring, safecracking, forgery), each from 0 to 1 with practice and learning curves
   (fast early, slow later).
2. **Ways to learn, by era:** on the job (a shift in a related job raises its skill), lessons and courses
   (correspondence courses, night school, a driving school; with costs from the economy's `priceIndex`, imported, not
   edited), books from the library, and being taught by a person (the game supplies who).
3. **Using a skill:** `attempt(skill, level, difficulty, seed, key)` gives success, failure or a botch (for the shady
   ones a botch makes noise or leaves evidence), deterministic from the key.
4. **What jobs need:** a mapping the jobs module can read later: role to required skill and level. Don't edit
   `src/jobs/`; export the table.
5. **Era gates:** hot-wiring has no meaning before electric starters, and safes change by era (combination, then time
   locks).
6. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/SKILLS.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- Practice raises a skill with diminishing returns.
- A course costs by era.
- `attempt` succeeds more often at higher levels.
- A botch on a lockpick reports noise.
- Era gates hold.
- Determinism.
