## Why it matters
Your Torrel (C017) is the game's favourite car to drive. The owner wants a souped-up version of it: a Torrel that has
been modified for racing, with a big rear wing, much better handling and more speed. It becomes a car the player can
choose when they spawn one, and later something they can find, buy or build up.

## Brief
Start from your **1988 Torrel** (`torrel-1988`) and make **`torrel-1988-rs`**: the same car after a racing shop got
hold of it. It must still read as a Torrel at a glance (same body, lamps, cabin and proportions), with clearly
bolted-on race parts:
- **a big rear wing** on tall uprights, well above the deck: the first thing anyone notices;
- **wider bolt-on arches** front and rear (riveted flares), with wider, lower tyres on light multi-spoke race wheels;
- **a front splitter** and a deeper front bumper with larger intakes; small canards are welcome;
- **lowered**: noticeably less ride height;
- **a stripped interior**: a roll cage, a racing bucket seat with harness, no passenger-side trim;
- **race details**: tow hooks, quick-release bonnet pins, a fuel filler on the bonnet or roof, a side-exit or centre
  exhaust, a racing number roundel and a livery of your own design (stripes or blocks of colour) in the paint mask.

Keep it **original**, as in C017: no real makers, sponsors, logos or real liveries. Make up a tuning shop name for the
decals if you like.

## Must have
- **Everything in the vehicle rules in C011** (docs/queue/tasks/C011.md, "Vehicle rules"): nodes, lights, paint mask,
  interior, collision, mid and far models, and the review sheet. The wing and the arches must be in the collision
  shape and in the mid and far models.
- **Same pixel-art texture style and texel size as the Torrel** (`texel_m` 0.0388), so it sits next to it.
- **Handling numbers in model-info.json**, as a race-modified car of the late 1980s:
  - mass (lighter than the road car, from the stripped interior);
  - weight distribution front/rear;
  - wheelbase and track (the track wider, from the arches);
  - tyre width and radius, front and rear;
  - downforce (the wing's and splitter's, at 100 and 200 km/h, as a rough figure);
  - ride height;
  - top speed and 0-100 km/h;
  - a suggested final drive and gear ratios.
- **Paint options:** two liveries of your own design plus the plain palette.
- **Review sheet:** the race car next to the road Torrel from the side, a low front three-quarter "hero" shot, the wing
  from behind, and the interior with the cage.

Work in `prototypes/torrel-1988-rs/` only. Write DELIVERY.md as the queue README says, with the Self-review from TEAM.md.
