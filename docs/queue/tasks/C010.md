# C010: Pawn shop and second-hand store

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: model
- Branch: `codex/C010-pawn-shop`
- Status: hold

## Why it matters
Part of the first playable loop (docs/queue/TEAM.md): it's where the player sells what they own, or what they stole.
The game's money and crime systems use it for buying, selling and fencing goods.

## Brief
An original pawn shop on a side street of the capital (made-up name on a repaintable sign), through the eras:
1. **1920-1959:**
   - a narrow brick storefront with three golden balls hanging over the door, and "LOANS" lettered on the window;
   - a front room crowded with instruments, tools and radios on shelves;
   - a glass jewellery counter, and a tall back counter with a brass cage window where the clerk sits;
   - a safe in the back office.
2. **1960-1999:**
   - the same shop with neon-style "PAWN" and "GUNS - GOLD - TV" signs;
   - burglar bars on the windows, a buzzer door;
   - a back storeroom of tagged items on racks.
3. **2000 onward:**
   - modernised: an LED "WE BUY GOLD" sign, security cameras (small nodes), a bulletproof clerk window, electronics and
     game consoles on shelves;
   - or a "closed, for lease" variant.

## Must have
- **Nodes:**
  - the counter as a named node (`counter`), with the clerk's spot and the customer's spot as anchors;
  - the safe (`safe`), the back door (`door_back`), the storeroom door (`door_store`), and the window bars (the
    escape and break-in features, for the crime system);
  - shelves as a few named display zones (`display_*`) where the game can place item props.
- **Collision:** walls, the counters and the shelves.
- **Night:** a lit sign and a dim night-light inside.
