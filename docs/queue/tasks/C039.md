## Why it matters
The capital has a movie theater with era posters. A town's evenings and weekends need things to do and spend money on,
and they draw crowds (more eyes) or empty the streets (fewer). A ball game on the radio (your news module) needs its
fixtures, and a dance hall is where people meet.

**The player's first encounter:** Friday 1952, the Strand shows a double feature at 7 and 9, 50¢. The dance hall's band
starts at 8. Saturday's ball game at 2 means the streets near the park fill at 1:30 and empty Main Street.

## What to build: `src/entertainment/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
- **Cinema:** programmes by era (silent, talkies from about 1928, double features, drive-ins from 1933, with authored
  film titles in genre styles, never real films), showtimes by weekday, and ticket prices via the economy's
  `priceIndex` (import it; don't edit `src/economy/`).
- **Dance halls, clubs and bars with music** by era: what nights and times, cover charges.
- **Sports:** fixtures for the town's team by season (your calendar: import it), with results from `rand` that the
  news module can report.
- **Crowds:** `crowdAt(place, year, day, hour, seed)` gives how many people are at each venue and in the streets
  around it, so the game can place bystanders and empty other streets.
- Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/ENTERTAINMENT.md`. No Node or browser APIs, no `Date.now` or
  `Math.random`.

## Tests
- No talkies before 1928.
- Drive-ins only from their era.
- Game-day crowds near the park and fewer on Main Street.
- Prices by era.
- Determinism.
- At least one test per bullet above.
