## Why it matters
Gossip travels through people (Jeff's crime code), and people meet somewhere: Sunday service, the lodge's Thursday
night, the bowling league, the PTA, the union hall. Who belongs where shapes who hears what, who vouches for whom, and
where the player can meet the town. The capital has a church; it has no social life yet.

**The player's first encounter:** in 1952, Sunday service at the brick church fills Main Street at 10:45. Afterwards
the deacon mentions to the player's landlady that "a new face was asking about the hardware store's back door".

## What to build: `src/community/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
- **Groups by era:** congregations, fraternal lodges, a veterans' post (after the wars), unions (by era), sports
  leagues, the PTA, a women's club, the chamber of commerce. Each has meeting times on your calendar (import it; don't
  edit it), a place kind, and dues by era (the economy's `priceIndex`).
- **Membership:** `membership(people, town, year, seed)` assigns the town's people (the game supplies traits, age, job,
  religion where known, wealth) to groups plausibly. It returns plain lists and each group's officers.
- **Ties for gossip:** `tiesFrom(membership)` gives extra relationship edges, weighted by how often members meet, in the
  shape Jeff's gossip code takes (an affinity from 0 to 1 between person ids; read `src/crime/types.ts`, don't edit it).
- **Joining:** what it takes for the player to join each group (sponsors, dues, standing in town) and what membership
  gives (introductions, a reference, a vouch with police by era).
- Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/COMMUNITY.md`. No Node or browser APIs, no `Date.now` or
  `Math.random` (use `rand`).

## Tests
- Meetings land on their calendar days.
- Veterans' posts appear after their wars.
- Ties are stronger for weekly groups than for yearly ones.
- Joining a lodge needs a sponsor.
- Determinism.
