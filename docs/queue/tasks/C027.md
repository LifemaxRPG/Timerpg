# C027: Weather that comes from the season

## Why it matters
The game can draw clear skies, overcast, rain, mist and snow, but the weather only changes when the owner taps a
button. Real weather comes from the season and the place, and it shapes the loop: fog and rain hide a thief from
witnesses, snow slows the walk and fills the diner, a heat wave empties the street at noon.

**The player's first encounter:** a Mistmonth evening, fog rolls in off the river after 6 p.m. The witness across the
street sees "someone in a dark coat" instead of the player's face.

## What to build: `src/weather/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Climate by season and region:** a few authored climate kinds (temperate coast, inland plains, mountain, warm
   south). Each gives monthly temperature ranges, chances of rain, snow and fog, and storm seasons. Use your calendar
   (`src/calendar/`: months and seasons; import it, don't edit it).
2. **Hour-by-hour weather:** `weatherAt(climate, dayNumber, hour, seed)` gives the sky (clear, cloudy, overcast, rain,
   storm, snow, fog), temperature, wind and visibility in metres. Weather systems last hours to days (fronts), not
   flips every hour. Deterministic from the seed.
3. **Effects for the game:** `weatherEffects(w)` gives how far a witness can make out a face, a walking-speed modifier,
   how busy the streets are, and a car grip modifier.
4. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/WEATHER.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- Snow only in the cold months of cold climates.
- Fog cuts visibility.
- Fronts persist across hours.
- Sensible monthly temperature ranges for each climate (state them).
- Determinism.
