# G018: Fast timeline replays (sim checkpoints and a real arson verb)

- Assignee: gemini
- Type: engineering (TypeScript)
- Branch: `gemini/G018-sim-checkpoints`
- Status: accepted (review r1)

## Why it matters
Your G017 timeline engine works, but a cold jump from 1952 to 2025 in the capital takes about 700 ms. About 70% of that
is `runTimeline` in `src/sim/simulate.ts`:
- it clones the whole world every year;
- it advances the settlement through decades that no intervention touched.

On the phone that will be slower still. And arson is modelled as 12 sabotage hits, so the chronicle and the newspaper
never say a store burned down. This task carries out your own proposal.

**The player's first encounter:** the player burns the hardware store in 1952 and jumps to 2025. The new present is
ready almost before the jump's flash fades, and the 1952 Gazette in the library reads "Hardware store burns down".

## What to build (one deliverable)
1. **Checkpoints in `src/sim/simulate.ts`:** add an optional `checkpoint?: World` (plus its year) to `RunOptions`, so a run
   resumes from a cached world instead of replaying untouched years. Also add `onlyFinalSnapshot?: boolean`, which skips
   the intermediate clones. Both are opt-in: existing callers and tests behave exactly as before.
2. **A native `arson` intervention** in `src/sim/types.ts` and `simulate.ts`:
   - it closes the business in that year with one chronicle event, "<Store> burned down";
   - it can hurt or kill people inside at night, by a seeded chance;
   - it leaves the lot empty to be rebuilt by the settlement's normal rules.
3. **Use both in `src/timeline/cache.ts`:**
   - resume from the baseline's snapshot at the year before the earliest intervention;
   - map arson to the new verb;
   - add a small LRU cap on cached runs (16 entries; the phone has limited memory).
4. **Fix the timing test** so it logs the time and asserts only cache hit against cache miss, never wall-clock thresholds.

## Tests
- With and without a checkpoint, the same seed and interventions give the same world: compare `diffWorlds`, which must
  be empty.
- A cold 1952-to-2025 jump in the capital takes under 200 ms on your machine (logged, not asserted), and you state what
  you measured.
- Arson writes one "burned down" event and closes the store in that year.
- The LRU cap evicts the oldest entry.
- All existing `src/sim`, `src/timeline`, `src/crime`, `src/police` and `src/court` tests stay green.
