# C011: Cars, set 1: 1920s to 1950s

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: model
- Branch: `codex/C011-cars-early`
- Status: accepted

## Why it matters
The owner wants a way to get around once the first playable loop works. These are the first drivable vehicles; the
game adds driving and physics (Jolt) on top. Cars appear across the century because of time travel.

## Brief
Three original cars:
1. **A late-1920s touring sedan:** tall and upright, running boards, separate fenders, spoked wheels, a spare on the
   back, a flat windscreen, black or deep colours.
2. **A late-1930s rounded coupe:** pontoon fenders, a tall grille, a long hood, a sloped back.
3. **A mid-1950s family sedan:** two-tone paint, chrome bumpers and trim as pixel art, a wraparound windscreen, small
   tail fins, whitewall tyres.

## Vehicle rules (all vehicle tasks)
- **The look:** our pixel-art style on simple low-poly bodies (sharp nearest-filtered pixel textures on the 0.0388 m
  texel grid, lit and shadowed like the buildings), not smooth high-poly cars. Each must read at a glance as its era and
  kind from 30 m away.
- **Original designs only:** no real makes, models, badges, grilles or trade dress. Made-up brand names and badges are
  welcome (e.g. a made-up maker per era), and must be repaintable per vehicle.
- **Units and axes:** glTF 2.0 `.glb`, metres, Y up, **forward is +Z**, the origin on the ground at the centre between
  the axles. Real proportions: a person is 1.75 m.
- **Nodes the game drives:**
  - `wheel_fl`, `wheel_fr`, `wheel_rl`, `wheel_rr` (motorcycles: `wheel_f`, `wheel_r`), each pivoted at its hub. The
    front wheels and the handlebars steer: `steer_fl`, `steer_fr` or `handlebar` as parent pivots.
  - `door_*` hinged at their hinge line, `hood`, `trunk` (where the body has them).
  - `steering_wheel`.
  - `seat_driver` and `seat_*` anchors, where a 1.75 m person sits; motorcycles: `seat_rider`, `seat_pillion`.
  - `exit_left` and `exit_right` anchors.
  - Light nodes, with an emission mask covering only the lamp faces: `light_head_*`, `light_tail_*`, `light_brake_*`,
    `light_turn_*`, and `light_reverse_*` where the era has them.
- **Body colour:** a body-paint mask so the game can repaint each car; give 4-6 era-typical colours in model-info.
- **Interior:** a simple, readable interior: seats, dash, steering wheel and gauges as pixel art. Windows show it.
- **Collision:** a few boxes for the body (in glTF metres, listed in model-info.json), plus the wheel radius and
  track, the wheelbase, the mass estimate and the top speed of the era, for the physics we add later.
- **Distance models:** a mid model (under 40% of the triangles) and a far model (under 150 triangles) for each vehicle.
- **Budget:** up to 3,000 triangles per car and 2,000 per motorcycle; at most 6 materials.
- **Review sheet, per vehicle:**
  - front three-quarter, rear three-quarter, side and top views;
  - at night with its lights on;
  - doors open, the interior through the driver's door;
  - a 1.75 m person standing beside it for scale.
- Work in `prototypes/<name>/` only. DELIVERY.md as the queue README says, with the Self-review from TEAM.md.
