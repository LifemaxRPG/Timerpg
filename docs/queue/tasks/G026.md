## Why it matters
Your own survey answer, taken: the loop returns pay, bail, fees and fines but leaves cash, clothes and inventory to the
caller, so its pieces feel separate. Build the player's life as one state inside the loop.

## What to build (in `src/loop/`)
1. **The player's state in `LoopSim`:** cash in integer cents, carried items (with where they came from: bought,
   stolen, found, carried through time), owned and worn clothes, lodging (rented room or home, rent due), and daily
   bills (food, rent, car running costs from Sam's economy in `src/economy/`, by era).
2. **Everything acts on it:** shifts pay into it; stops, arrests, bail, fines, court fees (G019) and sentences take
   from it; a time jump (G020) carries the player and their things but not era-bound things (a rented room, a bank
   balance); sales, thefts and pawning move items.
3. **Clothes as a disguise:** what the player wears is part of the description witnesses give (G005/G009); changing
   clothes weakens a match, as you suggested. Buying clothes fits the era's shops.
4. **Bills that bite:** unpaid rent leads to a warning, then a lockout, as in Sam's C022 rules where they exist.
5. Keep every public signature; add calls; document them in `docs/LOOP_API.md`; plain JSON for the bridge.

## Tests
- A day of work, a theft, a stop and a fine leave exactly the right cash and items.
- A clothes change weakens a witness match; the same clothes keep it.
- A time jump keeps carried things and drops the rented room.
- Unpaid rent gives a warning then a lockout. A save round-trips and continues identically.
