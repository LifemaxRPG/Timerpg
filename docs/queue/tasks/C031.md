## Why it matters
Word of mouth spreads crime news person to person (Jeff's code). A town also has a newspaper and, from the 1920s, the
radio. They make the world feel alive: prices, weather, the ball game, a fire on Elm Street, and sometimes the
player's own crime, written up with the witnesses' description. The owner wants era radio in the car on long drives.

**The player's first encounter:** the morning after the hardware store break-in, the 1952 Capital Courier's page 3
reads "Thief Takes Tools from Elm St. Hardware; Police Seek Man in Grey Coat". The diner radio plays the farm report,
then a dance band.

## What to build: `src/news/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **The paper:** `edition(town, day, events, year, seed)` builds the day's paper from the events the game supplies
   (crimes with their public description, fires, deaths, openings and closings of shops, weather, prices, holidays from
   your calendar). It returns plain JSON: headlines and short items, sized and placed by importance, in the era's tone
   (taking your opening as the model). It includes filler items (social notes, sport results, ads using the economy's
   prices) so the paper is never empty. A crime appears only once it's reported, and the paper's details come from what
   the police and witnesses know, so it can be wrong.
2. **The radio:** `radioAt(year, hour, weekday, seed)` gives what's on (news on the hour, music by era's style, serials,
   farm reports, ball games; music genre names only, no real songs or artists), with station call signs invented for our
   world.
3. **Era gates:** no radio before about 1922, television news later (just flag it for the future), and the paper's price
   by era.
4. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/NEWS.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs, no
   `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- A reported crime makes the paper with the reported description, and an unreported one doesn't.
- Radio exists only from its era.
- The paper's price changes by era.
- Determinism.
