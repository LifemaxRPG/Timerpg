## Brief
Two models.
(a) A one-room rural schoolhouse (about 1890-1950): white clapboard, a bell cupola, a woodstove, outhouses, a pump.
(b) A 1955 one-storey brick elementary school: a long classroom wing with ribbon windows, a gym-cafeteria, a
playground (swings, slide, monkey bars, a four-square court), a flagpole, bike racks, a bus loop.
States for (b): 1955-1989 as built; 1990+ with portables and a secure entrance; (a) states: in use, then abandoned.
