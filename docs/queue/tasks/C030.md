# C030: Lodging and food places across the capital

## Why it matters
Your needs (C026) make the player eat and sleep, and your cost of living (C022) prices meals and rooms. The game still
needs the places themselves: what's on a diner's menu by era, what a lunch counter or an automat or a drive-in serves,
and what a hotel, a boarding house and a flophouse offer. A menu is also where the era shows best.

**The player's first encounter:** the Junction Diner's 1952 menu board: coffee 10¢, a blue-plate special 65¢, apple pie
20¢. The night clerk at the Station Hotel quotes $2.50 a night, bath down the hall.

## What to build: `src/places/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Menus by era and kind:** diner, lunch counter, automat (its era), drive-in (its era), cafeteria, bakery, and a bar
   or tavern (with Prohibition-era rules: none from 1920 to 1933 except a speakeasy). Items with prices (use the
   economy's `priceIn` where items exist, plus your own menu items priced through `priceIndex`) and how much hunger each
   relieves (in C026's meal terms: diner meal, snack...). `menuOf(kind, year, seed)`.
2. **Lodging offers:** hotel classes (grand, commercial, station hotel, flophouse, motel by era) with rooms, what's
   included and the nightly price. `lodgingOffers(kind, year, seed)` builds on C022's `lodgingOf` where it fits.
3. **Opening hours** for these kinds through your `hoursOf` (diners early, bars late, automats all day), as a mapping;
   don't edit the economy, but document the kinds the game should pass.
4. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/PLACES.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs, no
   `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- No automat or drive-in outside its era.
- No legal bar during Prohibition (a speakeasy only).
- The 1952 diner prices sit in a sensible range (state it).
- Menus are deterministic by seed.
- Every menu item maps to a C026 meal kind.
