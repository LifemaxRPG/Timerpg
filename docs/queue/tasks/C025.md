# C025: The calendar (weekdays, seasons, paydays and the world's holidays)

## Why it matters
Your opening hours need a weekday and a date, and the game has neither: it counts days from the start and assumes the
first day is a Sunday. Holidays can't apply. Paydays, rent days, market days, seasons and school terms all hang on a
calendar. The game's world is its own planet, so its holidays should be the world's own, not real-world ones.

**The player's first encounter:** it's the 14th of Harvestmonth, a Friday and a payday, and the shops stay open late.
Tomorrow is the Lantern Festival: the bank and the post office are shut, and the square is full.

## What to build: `src/calendar/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **Dates:** `dateOf(dayNumber, startDate)` gives year, month, day, weekday, season and week of year. Use a 12-month,
   7-day-week calendar with names authored for our world (keep weekday names familiar, so the game reads easily). Plus
   `dayNumberOf(date)`.
2. **The world's holidays:** an authored list (about 8-12 by era: some appear, some fade), each with its date rule (a
   fixed date, or "the third Monday of …"), what closes (banks, post office, most shops, schools) and what opens (fairs,
   parades). `holidaysOn(date, year)`.
3. **Rhythms:** paydays by era (weekly Friday pay early on, every other week later), market days, school terms, and
   daylight hours by season (sunrise and sunset, for shop lighting and crime at night).
4. Your `hoursOf` takes a `date` already: give it a `ShopDate` adapter so the game passes the calendar straight in. Add
   it in `src/calendar/`; don't edit `src/economy/`.
5. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/CALENDAR.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random`.

## Tests
- Day numbers round-trip to dates; weekdays cycle.
- A holiday with a "third Monday" rule lands correctly across years.
- Paydays change by era.
- Daylight is longer in summer.
- Determinism.
