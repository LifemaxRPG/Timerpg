# C026: Hunger, sleep and health (the player's needs)

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C026-needs`
- Status: accepted

## Why it matters
Your cost of living (C022) prices meals and a room, but nothing in the game makes the player eat or sleep. Needs are
what turn money into survival, and give the day a rhythm: a meal at noon, a bed at night, a doctor when it goes wrong.
Kept light, as in a good life sim, not a chore: needs nudge, they don't nag.

**The player's first encounter:** after a night out and no breakfast, the player is "hungry and tired". Their walking
pace drops a little, and the diner's 35¢ breakfast feels worth it. A fall from a fence leaves a sprain, and the doctor's
visit is $3 in 1952.

## What to build: `src/needs/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **State:** hunger, fatigue, hygiene and health from 0 to 1, in plain JSON. `tick(state, hours, activity)` advances
   them by activity (walking, running, working, sleeping, idle), with gentle rates: about three meals and one sleep a day
   keeps them fine.
2. **Relief:** `eat(state, meal)` (diner meal, home-cooked, a snack, boarding-house board: tie them to C022's meal kinds
   by name), `sleep(state, hours, bedQuality)` (a bed, a park bench, a cell), `wash(state)`.
3. **Effects:** `effects(state)` gives plain modifiers the game applies: walking speed, how people react to the player
   (unwashed, exhausted), and the chance of dozing off when exhausted. Plus labels such as "hungry", "exhausted",
   "starving".
4. **Health and injury:** minor injuries (a sprain, a cut, a bruise; from falls, fights, a car crash at speed) heal over
   days, faster with treatment. `treatmentCost(injury, year)` by era uses the economy's `priceIndex` (import it from
   `src/economy/`). Illness can come from exhaustion and starvation.
5. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/NEEDS.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- A day with three meals and a night's sleep stays healthy.
- Skipping meals reaches "hungry" and then "starving" on a known schedule.
- Sleep in a cell or on a bench restores less than a bed.
- A sprain heals faster with treatment.
- Treatment costs scale by era.
- Determinism.
