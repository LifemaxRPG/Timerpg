## Brief
The Plains skyline: a row of 6-8 tall concrete grain silos (about 35-40 m) with a headhouse on top, a
conveyor gallery, a wooden 1920s elevator beside it, truck scale and office, and a rail siding loading spout
(rails are drawn by the game; give the siding's position in model-info.json).
States: 1925-1949 the wooden elevator alone; 1950-1989 wooden plus concrete silos; 1990+ concrete only, the wooden
one gone, a co-op sign. Faded painted name on the silos (repaintable region).
