# C019: Prices that move by category, and shelves that sell out

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C019-economy-curves`
- Status: open

## Why it matters
Your economy (C018) is in the game: shops, the pawn shop and shift pay all use it on the phone. Two things from your own
self-review and the game wiring come next:
- **One price index moves everything together.** In real history, electronics got cheaper while services got dearer.
  A 1978 calculator should cost a week's wages, a 2005 one pocket change.
- **Shelves never run out.** `stockOf` is a snapshot. Buying, or stealing, the last radio leaves it on the shelf, and
  the stock only changes because the day is in the seed.

**The player's first encounter:** the player buys the general store's only radio in 1952. The next day the shelf is
empty. A week later a new one is in, and the shopkeeper mentions the price went up.

## What to build (in `src/economy/` only)
1. **Relative price curves by category**, on top of the general index: a small authored table per category
   (electronics, appliances, clothing, food, fuel, services such as wages and meals, tools). Electronics fall steeply
   after their introduction years; services rise faster than goods. `priceIn` uses them.
2. **Shop stock with state:** a plain-JSON `ShopStock` the game keeps and saves.
   - `openStock(shopKind, year, seed)` returns it;
   - `take(stock, itemId, n)` returns the new stock (buying and stealing both call it);
   - `restock(stock, days, year, seed)` refills over time by each shop kind's delivery rhythm (the grocer daily, the
     hardware store weekly), deterministic from the seed;
   - plus how much the shop still holds of each item.
   The current `stockOf` stays working as before, so the game doesn't break.
3. **A JSON entry for each new function**, documented in docs/ECONOMY.md with an example.

## Tests (Vitest)
- A calculator's price falls from its first year to 2005, while a diner meal's price rises faster than the index.
- Buying the last of an item empties it until a restock.
- The restock rhythm differs between the grocer and the hardware store.
- Determinism by seed.
- The C018 tests stay green unchanged.
