# C017: A fun sports car (the owner's request)

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: model
- Branch: `codex/C017-sports-car`
- Status: open

## Why it matters
The owner asked for a cool car with great handling that's fun to drive around town and down the new highways and
mountain switchbacks. It is the first car built for the joy of driving. The game's physics (Jolt) will tune it from
your numbers.

## Brief
An **original** small sports coupe in the spirit of the classic European sports cars of the 1960s-70s:
- compact, low and light, with a short wheelbase;
- the engine behind the driver (rear or mid);
- a tight two-seat cabin and a driver-focused cockpit;
- a design that says "nimble" at a glance.

**It must be its own design, not a copy of any real car.** The owner mentioned a Porsche as inspiration for the
*feel*, so avoid that maker's signature cues:
- no frog-eye round headlamps on a sloping bonnet;
- no fastback teardrop roofline;
- no whale tail or "ducktail" in that shape;
- no real badges or names.

Find a fresh silhouette of your own. For example: a crisp wedge with pop-up or rectangular lamps, a targa roof, a
sharp Kamm tail, side air intakes ahead of the rear wheels. Give it a made-up maker and model name, with badges you
design.

**Two versions:**
1. **1968-1976:** chrome bumpers, narrow tyres, a wood-rim steering wheel, simple gauges, a targa panel that comes off
   (a `roof_panel` node).
2. **1988-1996:** a restyled successor with wider arches, a rear wing, deep-dish wheels, and a removable targa.

## Must have
- **Everything in the vehicle rules in C011** (docs/queue/tasks/C011.md, "Vehicle rules"): nodes, lights, paint mask,
  interior, collision, mid and far models, and the review sheet.
- **Handling numbers in model-info.json, carefully:**
  - mass (light: about 950-1,150 kg);
  - weight distribution front/rear;
  - wheelbase and track;
  - tyre width and radius, front and rear (staggered on the later car);
  - top speed and 0-100 km/h of the era;
  - a suggested final drive.
- **Bright paint options** in the palette: red, yellow, racing green, white, orange, a metallic.
- **Review sheet:** also include a low front three-quarter "hero" shot and the car on a twisty road.

Work in `prototypes/<name>/` only. Write DELIVERY.md as the queue README says, with the Self-review from TEAM.md.
