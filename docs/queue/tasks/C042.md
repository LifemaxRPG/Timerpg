# C042: Life on the road (roadside stops, hitchhikers and the journey)

- Branch: `codex/C042-road`

## Why it matters
The game has no fast travel: an hour's drive between cities is part of play (the owner: "it'll be a fun drive"). The
journey needs things that happen on it: a diner at a crossroads, a motel sign at dusk, a hitchhiker with a story, a
roadside fruit stand, a speed trap, fog in the hills. Your weather, cars and news modules already exist; this ties them
into the road.

**The player's first encounter:** 40 miles out of the capital in 1955, a hitchhiker in a pea coat waves by a mile
marker. He's heading to the port. Halfway there, the radio says a convict walked away from the county work farm that
morning, "last seen in a pea coat".

## What to build: `src/road/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
- **Roadside places by era along a road:** `roadsideAlong(road, year, seed)` takes a road the game supplies (length,
  kind: county road, highway or expressway, the towns at its ends, the terrain) and gives diners, motels, gas stations,
  fruit stands, tourist traps, rest areas and truck stops. Their spacing depends on the road kind and era (motels from
  the 1940s, rest areas on expressways).
- **Encounters:** `encountersOn(road, year, hour, weather, seed)` returns hitchhikers (by era: common until about the
  1970s), stranded drivers, speed traps (police by era), road work, livestock on rural roads and fog banks, each with a
  mile marker and plain facts. A hitchhiker has a destination and a short backstory, sometimes tied to the news (the game
  passes in news items).
- **Travel time and fatigue:** `driveTime(road, speedLimitByEra, weather)`, plus when a driver would stop to eat or
  sleep (your needs module, imported).
- Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/ROAD.md`. Import from `src/weather/`, `src/needs/` and
  `src/economy/`; don't edit them. No Node or browser APIs, no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand`).
