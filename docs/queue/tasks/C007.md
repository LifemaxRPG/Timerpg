## Brief
A 1960s roadside miniature golf course (18 holes on about 40 x 50 m) with fibreglass-style obstacles in our
pixel style: a windmill (its blades a node `windmill_blades` the game can spin), a lighthouse, a castle, a
covered bridge, a volcano, a little pond with a bridge, a clubhouse/ticket hut with a sign (made-up name).
Leave an open round pad (about 10 x 12 m) at the entrance for a big roadside statue the game will place there.
States: 1962-1989 open; 1990-2005 faded; 2006+ closed, overgrown, chain-link fence.
