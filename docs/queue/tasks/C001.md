## Brief
A two-storey brick Main Street store of about 1925 (about 10 x 28 m) that fits a street wall (party walls both
sides, finished back). Big display windows, a recessed entrance, a red-and-gold painted name band.
States: 1925-1969 family five-and-dime (long wooden counters and a soda/candy counter as fixed geometry);
1970-1999 same store, fluorescent dropped ceiling, plastic sign over the old band; 2000+ a discount dollar store
(made-up name) with window posters; and a closed/FOR LEASE variant.
