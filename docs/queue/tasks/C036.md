# C036: Houses for sale, mortgages and property tax

- Assignee: chatgpt
- Type: engineering
- Branch: `codex/C036-housing`
- Status: open

## Why it matters
The player can rent a room. The long game of legit money is a home of your own: a house bought with a mortgage from the
bank (your C033), with taxes, repairs and a value that rises and falls with the era and the street. A house is also
where the player keeps things (and a place to be robbed, or searched with a warrant).

**The player's first encounter:** in 1952 a two-bedroom bungalow on Elm Street is $7,800. The bank wants 20% down and
$42 a month for 20 years. The property tax is $96 a year, due in Harvestmonth.

## What to build: `src/housing/` (a new module of its own), pure and deterministic, with tests
1. **The market:** `housesForSale(homes, year, seed)` lists homes on offer (the game supplies homes with size, lot,
   condition and street), with prices by era, size, condition and neighbourhood (use the economy's `priceIndex`;
   import it, don't edit `src/economy/`), plus how long they've been on the market.
2. **Mortgages through the bank:** use your banking module's loan rules (import from `src/banking/`, don't edit it):
   down payment, rate and term by era, and monthly payments. Foreclosure after missed payments, on a known schedule.
3. **Owning:** property tax by era and value (due once a year on a calendar date; import `src/calendar/`), upkeep and
   repairs as the house ages, and a valuation that follows the era and the street.
4. Plain JSON in and out, documented in a new `docs/HOUSING.md` with an example of each call. No Node or browser APIs,
   no `Date.now` or `Math.random` (use `rand` from `src/sim/rng.ts`).

## Tests
- Prices scale by era and size.
- The mortgage payment is right for a known case (state it).
- Missed payments lead to foreclosure on schedule.
- Tax falls due on its date.
- Determinism.
