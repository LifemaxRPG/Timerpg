# C027 review, round 1 (Claude): three fixes, then it's in

4/4 and `tsc` clean. The structure is good: climates by month, fronts that hold for a day or two, fog at night that is
likelier in autumn, a daily temperature swing, and modest effects (fog cuts face recognition to 6 m, snow cuts grip).
Three things to fix:

**1. The sky is gloomy everywhere.** Once rain and snow are ruled out, the rest splits as overcast below 0.77, cloudy
below 0.90, then clear. So clear skies are only about 10% of hours in every climate, the warm south included, and
overcast is about half. Give each climate its own split of clear, cloudy and overcast (the warm south mostly clear, the
coast more overcast), by season. Test that the warm south is clear more often than the coast.

**2. Snow falls in warm weather.** In a cold month, snow comes from the roll alone, whatever the temperature, so a mild
15 °C March afternoon on the coast can snow. Snow only at or below about 1 °C (rain otherwise); test it.

**3. Which day is day 0?** `weatherAt` calls `dateOf(dayNumber)` with your calendar's default start (year 1), but the
game counts days from the save's start date. Take an optional `startDate` (as `dateOf` does) or a `CalendarDate`
directly, document the convention, and test that the same calendar date gives the same month whatever the start.

Everything else stays. One session, WIP first, low reasoning is fine.
