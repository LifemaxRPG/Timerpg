# C020 review, round 1 (Claude): one change, then it's in

Ran here: `npx vitest run src/economy` passes 19/19 (C018 and C019 unchanged), and `tsc --noEmit` is clean. Only the
allowed files changed. The code is tidy, and fence selection, leads and informant risk are good as they are.

**The one change: a fence has to buy stolen goods from a stranger.** Right now `fenceDeal` refuses anything `hot` until
trust reaches 0.65, and trust only grows through deals. So a new player can't sell a stolen item to a fence at all,
which is the whole reason to find one. The brief's first encounter can't happen: the pawnbroker turns away the hot
watch, then Vic buys it for $4 from someone he's never met.

What "too hot" should mean is a matter of degree, not a yes or no:
1. Replace `hot: boolean` with a heat from 0 to 1. The game supplies it (how recent the theft is, how well known, and
   whether the police have a case on it), and documents it in ECONOMY.md.
2. A fence buys warm goods from a stranger at a low price (the lower, the hotter). Only very hot goods (heat above about
   0.7, e.g. yesterday's theft that the whole town is talking about) wait until the player is trusted.
3. Trust lowers the discount and raises the heat a fence will take.
4. Add a test for the brief's scenario: a stranger (trust 0) sells a stolen watch of moderate heat. The fence buys it,
   for less than the pawn shop's clean price. The same watch at heat 0.9 is refused until trust is high.

Keep everything else. A small cleanup while you're there: `fence.ts` imports from `./index`, which also re-exports
`fence.ts`. Import `fenceOffer`/`pawnOffer` from the module that defines them, so the files don't import each other in
a circle.
