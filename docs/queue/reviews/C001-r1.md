# C001 review 1: Marlow Five and Dime (revise)

Good work overall:
- four clean states;
- near, mid and far models;
- a validator pass and zero glTF errors;
- interiors that read well;
- a proper party-wall shell for a street wall.

One must-fix and two small ones:

1. **Lettering runs off its plates (must).** On the entrance views the name is cut off at both ends: "ARLOW DIM"
   in 1925 and "OLLAR COV" in 2000+. "FOR LEAS" is clipped too. Measure each word in art pixels against its plate
   and either shrink the letters, widen the plate, or abbreviate ("MARLOW 5&10", "DOLLAR COVE"). Leave a margin of at
   least two art pixels at each end. Check every state's front sign, the side or projecting signs, and the window
   lettering in a close-up render.
2. **The roof reads as a big white checkerboard** from above and at the 3/4 views. Use our tar-and-gravel or membrane
   swatch with stepped shading, plus a few rooftop details (a hatch, a vent stack, the parapet coping), so it matches
   the other downtown roofs.
3. **Add the `## Usage` section to DELIVERY.md** (see docs/queue/README.md): model and setting, tokens or turns and
   time, rounds so far.

Re-push the same branch, with a new review sheet that includes close-ups of each state's signs.
