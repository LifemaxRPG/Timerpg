# C007 review, round 1 (Claude)

**Keep:**
- The name and the ticket hut.
- The 18 real cups, and the statue pad clear for the Rex.
- The windmill with its separate rotor, and the lighthouse.
- The three eras and the clean validation.

You called the main problem yourself: from above it reads as a parking lot of 18 identical green rectangles in rigid
rows. A roadside mini-golf is a little landscape, and the fun is in how it winds.

1. **Bigger and winding.** Take a 60 x 70 m parcel. Lay the holes as a meandering path that loops and doubles back,
   not rows. Every hole gets its own shape: doglegs, an S-curve, a figure-eight, a split green, a banked turn.
2. **Height and water:**
   - raised greens with rock walls;
   - a small hill the path climbs, with a downhill hole off it;
   - a stream with a little pond, the footbridge over it, and a waterfall over a rock ledge (as stepped pixel water);
   - one hole where the ball goes through a pipe down a level.
3. **Landscaping and colour:**
   - rockwork in big faceted boulders;
   - flower beds and shrubs, palms or pines in tubs, and `tree_*` nodes;
   - carpet greens in two or three colours (green, blue for the water holes, red for the volcano);
   - painted curbs;
   - hole-number signs on little posts;
   - benches, and lamp posts along the path.
4. **Bigger set pieces,** each a silhouette you'd remember:
   - the volcano taller, with a lava-red stripe;
   - the castle with a drawbridge tunnel;
   - a giant golf ball or a pirate ship as the hole-18 finale.
5. **The closed state must look closed:**
   - cracked, faded carpets with weeds poking through;
   - an empty pond and a dry stream bed;
   - the windmill with a missing blade;
   - graffiti on the castle;
   - a sagging fence with a gap in it;
   - tall grass taking the edges.
   Today it reads the same as open, plus a fence.
6. **Night (1962-1989):** coloured bulbs along the path, the windmill lit, and the lighthouse lamp glowing.
