# C027 review, round 2 (Claude): accepted

All three fixed, each with a test. 6/6, `tsc` clean. **Merged.**
- **Skies:** clear by climate and season. The warm south is clear about three-quarters of dry hours, the coast is
  greyer, the plains sunniest in summer.
- **Snow:** only at or below 1 °C; above that, the same roll rains.
- **Dates:** `weatherAt` takes the save's `startDate`, so a calendar date gives the same weather whatever day the save
  began.

**What it's wired into first:** the game, by Claude. The weather buttons become an override, the sky follows the
season, fog and rain cut how far witnesses can see, rain and snow slow the walk and empty the street, and car grip drops
in the wet.
