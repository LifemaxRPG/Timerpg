## Polish for the integration task

1. **Floating crest sheets.** In rough water, some crest highlights are big flat pale quadrilaterals (left of the boat
   in the close-ups). They read as paper sheets floating in the air. Break them into the "~" strokes and small
   whitecap clusters, the way the smaller crests already are.
2. **Stern dashes.** The white dashes behind the stern look like floating sticks. Make a wake: a V of broken foam
   that widens and fades behind a moving boat, and nothing when the boat is still.
3. **No moon road at night.** The night glint is now 0%. Bring back a sparse silver road under the moon: a few
   broken dashes, much fainter than dusk.
4. **The horizon stripe.** By day the distant-sea band is a bright saturated cyan stripe. Make it darker and greyer
   (aerial perspective), close to the sky's lowest band in value.
5. **Wide dither** still covers large patches of the near foreground in calm water. Keep transitions to 2-4 px.
6. **Measured vs oracle.** The preview measured the day glint at 0.00% while the CPU oracle says 4.27%. Make the
   oracle match the real shader, or drop it.
