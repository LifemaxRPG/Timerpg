# Purchased art packs (raw)

The full packs we bought, kept here so tools can cut new sprites and textures from them. The repo is private: keep it
that way. The licences allow using and editing the art in our games, not giving the raw art to others.

- `Modern_Farm_v1.3.zip` - LimeZu, Modern Farm (limezu.itch.io/modernfarm). Licence (LICENSE.txt in the zip): use and
  edit in commercial or non-commercial projects; no reselling or redistributing the asset; credits appreciated
  (limezu.itch.io). Has 16, 32 and 48 px versions; our town art is the 48 px scale.
- `Modern_Exteriors_v42.3.zip.part*` - LimeZu, Modern Exteriors (limezu.itch.io/modernexteriors). Licence (PDF in the
  zip): use and edit in commercial, non-commercial and open-source projects; no reselling or redistributing the asset
  (or NFTs); **credits required** (limezu.itch.io).
- `Modern_Interiors_v41.4.zip.part*` - LimeZu, Modern Interiors (limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors). Licence (LICENSE.txt):
  same terms; **credits required** (limezu.itch.io).

The two big zips are split into 90 MB parts (GitHub refuses files over 100 MB): `./join.sh <out-dir>` rebuilds them and
checks them against SHA256SUMS.
