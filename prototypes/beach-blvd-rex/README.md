# Rex — Jacksonville Beach Boulevard Dinosaur Statue (Est. 1964)

1-to-1 pixel-art 3D landmark model of **"Rex"**, the iconic ~20.6 ft tall orange-red roadside dinosaur statue standing at **10150 Beach Boulevard in Jacksonville, Florida** (originally built ca. 1964 as the mascot for Goony Golf miniature golf and restored in 2007), modeled directly from multi-angle photographs of the real statue.

Built to match the project's SNES-meets-PS2 pixel-art 3D style contract (`docs/CHATGPT_MODEL_GUIDE.md`).

## 1-to-1 Features Matched to Beach Blvd Photographs

- **Exact 1964 sculptural silhouette & proportions**: Big chunky rounded head (~23% of total height), sturdy forward-curving S-neck, plump pear-shaped lower torso, massive chunky hind legs, and thick ground-resting tail.
- **Smooth-vs-rough vermilion texture split**: Smooth painted vermilion orange-red plaster across the face, blunt snout, squared chin, front throat, and both cylindrical arms (`skin_rex_smooth`), paired with heavy organic pebbled vermilion stucco sweeping over the domed crown hood, back of neck, back, flanks, chunky legs, and tail (`skin_rex_rough`).
- **Signature facial landmarks**: Bold dark-outlined `'OO'` oval nostrils across the blunt snout front, narrow dark triangular wedge grin lined with uniform small white sawteeth, and deep-set dark eye sockets housing glowing ruby-red lightbulb eyes (`glow_rex_eyes` with strictly black-masked `glow_mask.png`).
- **Turquoise-teal teardrop belly patch**: Smooth coastal teal belly tucked cleanly beneath the arms with the statue's signature weathered warm-ochre center patina (`skin_rex_belly`).
- **Giant ~10 ft white femur bone cane**: Sculpted hourglass white concrete femur with plump double-lobed top & bottom condyles planted in the mulch (`3.04 m` tall), gripped on top by Rex's 3-fingered smooth left hand while his smooth right arm reaches forward ahead of the bone.
- **Chunky feet with 3 broad pale cream-yellow toenails**: Rounded cream-yellow dome toenails visible right through the fence pickets.
- **Actual 10150 Beach Blvd roadside site (`lot` node)**: Dark pine-bark mulch bed encircled by a tall **1.80 m (5.9 ft) black wrought-iron picket fence** (`lot_fence` alpha cutout + solid 3D iron posts), modest angled bronze `"REX / 1964"` historical plaque on a black iron post tucked inside the left fence beside Rex's left thigh, Florida grass apron, precast concrete street curb (`pavement` LimeZu swatch), Beach Blvd asphalt (`asphalt_worn` LimeZu swatch), and two low ground uplights.
- **Separated nodes**: `rex_statue`, `lot`, `collision` (3 boxes: legs/bone, ground tail, fence enclosure), and `door_main` ground interaction point at `(0, 0, -1.60)` in front of the fence.

