# C003 · Alder Bend Ballpark

Built an era-changing small-town baseball park with a covered 600-place wooden grandstand, line bleachers, two dugouts, a ticket booth, an open concession interior, an outfield fence with original local ads, a scoreboard and a gravel lot. The diamond uses 90-foot base paths. The 1950 transition adds four light towers to the wooden park. The 1960 model introduces steel bleachers, and the 2000 state adds aluminium seating, mown turf and a new scoreboard face.

All four dated states share the field, origin and open doorways. The delivery includes 12 GLBs, four packed Blender sources, reproducible scripts and a review sheet. The manifest lists up to 65 glTF-metre colliders for the full venue, field positions and repaintable sign rectangles.

## Verification

- All 12 GLBs passed the prototype validator for era features, open apertures, shared anchors, grade, collision boxes, LODs, physical UV scale, 2×2 art pixels and emissive pane masks. The largest asset has 7,015 visible triangles and 12 materials.
- Khronos glTF Validator reported zero errors and warnings across all 12 files. All four Blender sources opened with packed textures.
- Inspected the day, night, rear, overhead, gate, field and concession views in Blender Cycles. The sheet uses desktop renders.

Scoring and ticket logic, crowds, precise stand traversal, runtime floodlighting and Android integration remain game work. The installed scoreboard face is static.
