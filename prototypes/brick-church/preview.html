Town churches

Two original alternatives: a brick Gothic church with a thirty-metre corner spire and rear parish hall, or a compact white clapboard church with an open belfry and rear vestry. Both have actual curved window openings, clean naves and raised chancels. The game supplies pews and loose furnishings. Select the design from town facts; no congregation history, services or graves are invented. All views are Blender desktop renders, not Filament/S25 captures. Collision parsing, navigation, lighting, per-town names and interactions remain game integration work.
