# C001 · Five-and-dime

Built Marlow Five and Dime in four states with near, mid and far GLBs, editable packed Blender sources, original pixel textures and reproducible scripts. The prototype has a shared lot and door placement, ground-floor retail interiors, and sign repaint metadata. The review sheet includes six views per state.

- The prototype validator passed all 12 GLBs. It checks shared placement, door clearances, collision boxes, nearest sampling, 0.0388 m/texel and 2×2 texel artwork, pane emission and decreasing LOD triangle counts. The largest model has 2,724 visible triangles and 12 materials.
- Khronos glTF Validator reported zero errors and zero warnings across all 12 GLBs.
- Reviewed all four states together in the Blender Cycles contact sheet.

Android and Filament integration, door locking, and runtime LOD switching remain untested. The previews are Blender desktop renders.
