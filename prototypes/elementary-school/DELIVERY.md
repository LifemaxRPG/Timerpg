# C004 · Elementary school and one-room schoolhouse

Built Mill Branch School in active and abandoned states. It has white clapboard, a bell cupola, a woodstove and two outhouses by the schoolyard pump. The closed state has boarded windows, patched roofing and dark panes.

Built Westmeadow Elementary as it opened in 1955 and after its 1990 expansion. Both have the low brick classroom wing, ribbon windows, the gym-lunchroom, a bus loop, a flagpole and bike racks. The playground includes swings, a slide, overhead bars and a four-square court. The newer variant adds a glazed secure vestibule and two walk-in portable classrooms.
