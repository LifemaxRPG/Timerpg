Public Library

A 1912 brick-and-stone library with two reading rooms, central desk and a basement room reached by real stairs. A separate later-state GLB adds a low 1970s brick/glass wing through a short glazed link. Exterior thresholds stay open. These are actual Blender desktop views; basement navigation, terrain cutout, state selection and glTF collision parsing require game integration.
