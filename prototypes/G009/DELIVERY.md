# G009 Delivery — Police in Action (Patrols, Searches, Spotting, Arrests)

## Summary

Built `src/police/`, a pure, deterministic, plain-JSON police action engine joining the crime core (`src/crime/`, G005/G008) with the NPC plan language (`src/plan/`, G007). Officers patrol caller-supplied street graphs in shifts (foot or patrol cars with radio by era), respond to reported crimes to interview witnesses (refreshing G005 witness memory), search haunts and run stakeouts under high heat, spot wanted suspects and lookalikes via `recognitionChance`, call backup by era, pursue fleeing suspects (or halt at jurisdiction borders unless escalated), and execute full arrest/search/cuff/transport outcomes for downstream jail and trial systems.

No modifications were needed in `src/crime/` or `src/plan/`.

## Files Added

- `src/police/types.ts` — Plain-JSON state, street graph, officer, spotting, stop, interview, arrest, escape, and tick-context types.
- `src/police/force.ts` — Tunable `POLICE_ERA` table, `forceSizeForTown`, `buildBeats`, `shortestNodePath`, shift/heat helpers, and `createPoliceForce`.
- `src/police/plans.ts` — G007 plan builders for patrol, crime response + witness interviews, suspect search, stakeout, questioning/papers check, era-aware backup calls, foot/car chase, arrest, and prisoner transport, all compiled through `parsePlan` → `bind` → `createPlanState`.
- `src/police/spotting.ts` — Numeric-key `SpatialPersonIndex`, lighting & sight range helpers, lookalike resemblance filtering, `evaluateSpotting` (delegating to G005 `recognitionChance`), `decideSpotAction`, and `measureSpottingRate`.
- `src/police/engine.ts` — Pure tick driver `stepPoliceTick(state, ctx)` managing shifts, dispatch, witness memory refreshes, proactive heat search/stakeout, spotting, stops, backup dispatch, border escapes, and arrests/transport.
- `src/police/stubTown.ts` — Two-jurisdiction stub town street graph (`createStubTownGraph`) and `StubTownPlanWorld` implementing `PlanWorld`.
- `src/police/index.ts` — Barrel exports.
- `src/police/police.test.ts` — 9 deterministic scenarios covering all G009 test requirements, determinism/JSON round-trip, and the 30-officer × 2,000-citizen performance budget.
- `docs/POLICE.md` — Architecture and integration documentation.

## Self-review (per `docs/queue/TEAM.md`)

1. **What could still go wrong or what assumptions did I make?**
   - `StubTownPlanWorld` moves actors in straight lines between consecutive street-graph waypoints generated by `shortestNodePath`; real town integration will provide its own `PlanWorld` with building collision and doorway checks, which may return `blocked_locked` or `blocked_path` (currently handled by aborting a blocked chase back to patrol rather than dynamically routing around a locked gate).
   - `stepPoliceTick` requires plan-safe lowercase `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*` ids for witnesses, suspects, and street nodes so they pass G007 `parsePlan` and `bind`. If `World.people` uses colon-delimited ids like `p:0001`, the game adapter will need to map them to plan-safe aliases when constructing `PlanWorld` (or relax G007's identifier character class in a future revision).
   - Car chases re-plan to the suspect's current street node whenever the suspect changes nodes; on very sparse street graphs with long edges, a fleeing suspect who reverses mid-edge could cause a brief overshoot until they enter a different node.

2. **Did I cut any corners?**
   - No shortcuts on G009 requirements: every officer behaviour compiles through real G007 `parsePlan` + `bind` + `step`, witness interviews refresh real G005 `CrimeState` memory, spotting delegates to G005 `recognitionChance`, lookalikes get stopped and cleared, fleeing suspects are either chased/searched/cuffed/transported or escape across jurisdiction lines, 1925 vs 1990 differs in force size/transport/radio backup, and 30 officers in 2,000 people stay under 1 ms/tick.
   - One deliberate boundary: once an arrest completes `transportedToStation: true`, booking into jail / enrolling fingerprints in G008 `identityFiles` / launching a G010 trial is left to the caller consuming `events.arrests` so `src/police/` stays cleanly decoupled from jail & court rules.

3. **What was harder than expected, and what did I learn?**
   - Profiling the 30-officer × 2,000-citizen benchmark revealed a real gameplay bug hiding inside a performance problem: when the spotting pre-screen allowed any citizen sharing a single trait (e.g. "tall") to hit `recognitionChance`, 25 of 30 officers immediately stopped 66 random citizens in 20 seconds! Requiring multi-trait visual resemblance (`appearanceMatchScore >= 0.42`) for unnamed strangers fixed both realism (genuine lookalikes still get stopped by mistake, ordinary citizens don't) and cut tick time cleanly under 0.75 ms.
   - Checking jurisdiction border crossings during BOTH `chase` and `call_backup` (not just `chase`) mattered for fast-fleeing suspects who cross a county line during the 1-second radio callout window.

## Ideas for the Team

- **Booking pipeline bridge (G009 → G008 → G010):** When `ArrestRecord.transportedToStation` turns `true`, automatically calling G008 `enrollOnFile` (booking prints/DNA in the current era) means arresting a suspect for a minor 1982 offence can immediately crack their unsolved 1965 cold case on the next tick — linking G008, G009, and G010 into one emergent loop.
- **Roadblocks & APB checkpoints at border nodes (`kind === 'border'`):** When jurisdiction heat exceeds `70` on a serious escalated felony, dispatch a patrol unit to `watch` / check papers at `east_gate` border nodes so escaping to the next town becomes a tense stealth or bribe challenge rather than a free sprint.
- **Officer shift handoff briefing:** When a `day` officer clocks off and a `swing` officer starts shift at `station_a`, passing the active hot-node and recent-sighting tip during handoff would create visible continuity across shifts without omniscient telepathy in pre-radio eras.
